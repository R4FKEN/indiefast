# Growth Tools for Indie Makers

Most tool directories throw every growth product into one giant category and expect you to figure out the rest. That's not how real businesses grow. Once you've launched, started earning revenue, and worked through the [Measure stage](https://indiefa.st/blog/measure), growth becomes several different jobs happening at the same time. You're improving retention, finding new customers, building systems, supporting existing users, and creating repeatable acquisition channels—all at once. That's why IndieFast treats growth as six parallel functions instead of one catch-all bucket. Each function has a different goal, a different workflow, and a different tool we'd recommend. That structure helps founders make faster decisions instead of getting buried under another list of twenty unrelated products.

## Why Growth Is Parallel, Not Sequential

The first part of the IndieFast journey follows a clear order.

You discover an idea.

Research it.

Validate it.

Plan it.

Design it.

Build it.

Launch it.

Measure it.

Each stage naturally leads to the next.

Growth doesn't work like that.

You don't finish SEO before starting community.

You don't complete automation before thinking about support.

You don't master distribution and then begin scaling.

These six functions run together.

Every week you'll probably improve your automation, answer customer questions, publish content, refine your distribution strategy, strengthen your community, and prepare your infrastructure for more users.

That's what **how to grow an indie SaaS** actually looks like.

Growth isn't a checklist.

It's a collection of systems working together.
